# ❄️ Family White Christmas 🎅

### *A very secret, very festive Santa operation for the Tsogtbaatar, Dagvadorj, Lee, Ganbold & Bilguun crews*

---

Somewhere between the eggnog and the group chat chaos, gift-giving for a family this big got complicated. So this app exists to fix that: **sign up, build a wishlist, get matched with your secret person, and don't blow your cover.** 🤫🎁

No spreadsheets. No "wait, who has who again?" texts. Just snow, a snowman mascot, and one well-kept secret per person.

## 🎄 What it actually does

- **Sign up & sign in** — real accounts with Firebase Auth (email + password), because even Secret Santa deserves proper security
- **Build your wishlist** — add what you're hoping for so your Santa isn't guessing
- **Get matched, automatically** — names are drawn on **Thanksgiving, Nov 26, 2026 at 7:00 PM**, with a live countdown ticking down on the page. No manual "draw names" button, no peeking early, no rigging it (sorry, Uncle)
- **Everyone's matched outside their own family** — so no easy outs by gifting your sibling
- **Budget: $100** — keep it generous, keep it fair
- **Pick a profile frame** — 10 animated, glowing frames to choose from (5 masculine, 5 feminine) because Secret Santa should also be a little bit extra
- **Light mode & Night mode** — complete with a snowman who's along for the ride either way ⛄
- **English + Mongolian** — flip the language toggle, because this is a bilingual family
- **Gift status warnings** — a friendly nudge shows up if someone hasn't picked a gift yet, so nobody's left hanging on Christmas morning

## 🧑‍🎄 How a family member uses it

1. Sign up with your email and pick your name from your family's list
2. Build your wishlist (the more specific, the better — no one wants to guess your sock size)
3. Watch the countdown to Thanksgiving night
4. Get your secret match when the clock hits zero
5. Shop, wrap, and show up on Christmas with the goods 🎁

## 🛠️ Built with

A static site (hosted on GitHub Pages) backed by **Firebase Authentication** and **Firestore** — no servers to babysit, no hosting bills, just holiday magic running on Google's infrastructure. See [gift-exchange/README.md](gift-exchange/README.md) for the technical setup, Firebase config, and organizer/admin instructions.
